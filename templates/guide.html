Pregnancy Milestones Timeline

Week 4-5

Positive Pregnancy Test

Baby's neural tube begins forming, which will become brain and spine

Week 8

First Ultrasound

Baby's heartbeat can be detected, major organs begin developing

Week 12

End of First Trimester

Risk of miscarriage decreases significantly, nausea often improves

Week 20

Anatomy Scan

Detailed ultrasound to check baby's development and often reveal gender

Week 28

Third Trimester Begins

Baby's kicks become stronger, glucose screening test performed

Week 37+

Full Term

Baby could arrive any day now! Final preparations for birth

Delivery Options

Vaginal Delivery

  • Most common and natural method
  • Typically shorter recovery time
  • May include episiotomy if needed
  • Can use pain relief options

Cesarean Section

  • Surgical delivery through abdomen
  • Planned or emergency procedure
  • Longer recovery period
  • Used for high-risk pregnancies

Induced Labor

  • Medically started labor
  • Used when pregnancy continues past due date
  • Methods include Pitocin or breaking waters
  • Can lead to faster, more intense contractions
Health Essentials
  • Prenatal vitamins with folic acid
  • Balanced diet with extra 300 calories/day
  • Stay hydrated (8-10 glasses water daily)
  • Safe exercise (walking, swimming, yoga)
  • 7-9 hours of sleep nightly
Important Appointments
  • First prenatal visit (8 weeks)
  • NT Scan (11-14 weeks)
  • Anatomy scan (20 weeks)
  • Glucose test (24-28 weeks)
  • Group B strep test (36 weeks)
  • Weekly visits after 36 weeks

Myth vs Fact

Myth: You shouldn't exercise while pregnant

Fact: Moderate exercise is beneficial unless your doctor advises against it

Myth: Heartburn means your baby will have lots of hair

Fact: Heartburn is caused by hormones relaxing the esophageal sphincter

Myth: You're eating for two

Fact: You only need about 300 extra calories/day in later pregnancy
